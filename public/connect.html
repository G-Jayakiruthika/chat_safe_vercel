CHANNELS & VOICE IVR // 05

Use Chat_Safe × Urimai inside your apps & keypad phones

Forward a suspicious message on WhatsApp/Telegram/Instagram, OR link a rural parent's feature phone to Speed-Dial '9' Voice IVR (Twilio + Gemini 2.5 Flash) to trigger instant Kavach SOS or Urimai Action Kits.

Deploying Connectors (Google Cloud Run / Node.js)

Each connector switches on when its keys are set as environment variables on the server. Secrets never reach the browser.

Speed-Dial '9' Voice IVR (Twilio + Gemini)

  1. Configure a Twilio programmable voice number pointing to /api/agent.
  2. Rural elder dials 9 -> hears greeting in their chosen Indian language.
  3. Gemini classifies Scam (Kavach SOS) vs Civic (Urimai Action Kit) and pushes to Family Hub.

Telegram bot

  1. Create a bot with @BotFather and copy the token.
  2. Set TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET and TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME.

WhatsApp Business (Cloud API)

  1. Add WhatsApp Cloud API in Meta for Developers.
  2. Set META_APP_SECRET, META_VERIFY_TOKEN, WHATSAPP_TOKEN, WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID.
  3. Callback URL: , subscribe to messages.

Instagram DMs & Messenger

  1. Connect Instagram professional account or Facebook Page to the Meta app.
  2. Use callback URL with HMAC X-Hub-Signature-256 verification.
