08 // QUESTIONS

FAQ — Scams, Button Phones & Remote Family Management

How does a button-phone user trigger Kavach scam defense or Urimai petitions?

They speed-dial '9' on any button phone and speak in their mother tongue. Gemini classifies cyber scams for Kavach (1930 defense) or village breakdowns for Urimai (automated legal petitions) and sends the kit to your smartphone or laptop.

Can I manage my family's village civic issues or scam alerts remotely from another city or college?

Yes! Anyone can manage rural scam alerts and village civic grievances remotely from any smartphone or laptop using Solution 1 (Digital-First Bypass: 1-click copy-paste into CM Helpline / Mudhalvarin Mugavari / CPGRAMS) or Solution 2 (Local Proxy: forward a secure WhatsApp upload link to a neighbor or e-Seva center).

Does the PDF support Tamil, Hindi and regional scripts?

Yes! Our Unicode-safe A4 PDF generator renders Official English + any of 23 Indian scripts, embeds the uploaded street photo, and adds the 5-resident signature block.

Does it work offline / without a client API key?

Yes. Zero API keys are needed in the browser: the app calls the Vercel Serverless Gemini Flash proxy (/api/gemini) automatically when a server key is present and falls back seamlessly to the built-in deterministic offline engine.
